# DAR mental map

DAR asks whether an analytical structure answers a reviewed question, including
its atomic components and runtime calculations. DAB owns reusable business
meaning. The shared facilitator owns the conversation/loop; the
[DAR elicitation skill](../../../.agents/skills/dar/eliciting-dar-requirements/SKILL.md)
owns the analytical phase. Missing concepts return to DAB with a specific gap.

First inspect definitions, then mappings, then selective data. Reuse facts,
dimensions and relationship outputs where their grain, time, population and
meaning fit. A correct rollup can be smaller than an atomic fact; a configurable
distinct metric may require identity detail that the rollup destroyed.

When approved outputs already answer the question, use
[answering analytics questions](../../../.agents/skills/shared/answering-analytics-questions/SKILL.md).
Its [USS reference](../../../.agents/skills/dar/references/uss-querying.md) applies
only to a bound USS surface. A consumption task does not require a new model.

The [analytical reference](../../../.agents/skills/dar/references/analytical-design.md)
owns dimension/history choices, assertable grain, temporal cardinality, provenance,
runtime sufficiency, reconciliation and the evidence-report convention. Do not
duplicate its decision tables here. The
[Daana reference](../../../.agents/skills/dar/references/daana-dar.md) owns compiler
applicability and local/connected/write boundaries; actual bindings and dated
findings live in the task root's Project context, not in this mental map.

After an approved design, [author declarations](../../../.agents/skills/dar/authoring-dar-specs/SKILL.md)
and [inspect structure](../../../.agents/skills/dar/validating-dar-structure/SKILL.md).
[Verify results](../../../.agents/skills/dar/verifying-dar-output/SKILL.md) with the
right oracle for that grain. [Operations](../../../.agents/skills/dar/running-dar-operations/SKILL.md)
require resolved readiness, an exact packet and the bound fresh authorization.
No implicit install/reset or manual emitted-SQL workaround.

Structural proof, synthetic results and deployed-data reconciliation are different
claims. A directory, successful compile, declared check or approval does not
prove deployment, enforced constraints or correct data. Qualify every untested
path without blocking useful local work that has adequate inputs.
